**Edouard de Ponnat** is an incoming PhD student in Electrical and Computer Engineering. Prior to JHU, Edouard studied at EPFL, ENS de Lyon, and ETH Zürich, where his academic journey developed around mathematics, neuroscience, machine learning, and dynamical systems. He is particularly interested in how mathematical and computational tools can be used to understand learning, neural dynamics, and intelligent behavior. Outside of research, Edouard used to work as a sound system engineer while pursuing his studies. He also enjoys cycling and listening to music.
